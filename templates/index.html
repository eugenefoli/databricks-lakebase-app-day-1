📊 How Stock Analysis Works

Click the "Analyze" button next to any stock to generate a data-driven buy/sell/hold recommendation based on 50 days of historical trading data.

What We Analyze:

  • Moving Averages (SMA) - Identifies price trends over 20 and 50 days. Price above MA = bullish 📈, below = bearish 📉
  • RSI (Momentum) - Measures if a stock is overbought (>70) or oversold (<30). Helps spot potential reversals
  • Volume - Confirms the strength of price movements. High volume = stronger signal

Recommendations Explained:

🟢 BUY - Strong upward trend, good momentum ⚫ HOLD - Mixed signals, wait for clarity 🔴 SELL - Downward trend or overvalued

The confidence score (0-100%) shows how strongly the indicators align. Higher confidence = stronger signal.

⚠️ Important: These are educational recommendations based on technical analysis only. They are NOT financial advice. Always do your own research and consult a financial advisor before making investment decisions.
